The case for SPF

What a foam roof does that patch-and-pray can't

SPF roofing goes on as one seamless layer with no seams to leak, and it bonds to concrete, wood, steel, or your existing roof — so we can often install right over what you have and skip the costly tear-off and landfill fees. It's lightweight, adds insulation value on top of your roof, and a reflective coat cuts cooling costs. One material, tapered to drain, sealed tight.

SEAMLESS

Foam roofing — FAQ

Can you install a spray foam roof over my existing roof?

Often yes — SPF bonds to most existing low-slope roofs after prep, so we can roof over what you have and skip the tear-off and landfill fees. We'll assess yours first and tell you straight.

Does a spray foam roof add insulation?

Yes — a spray foam roof adds insulation value on top of your roof while it seals it, and a reflective top coat helps cut cooling costs. You're re-roofing and insulating in one step.
