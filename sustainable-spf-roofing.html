Sustainable roofing — FAQ

What makes a spray foam roof a sustainable choice?

A few things: it usually installs over the existing roof, so the old roof stays out of the landfill; it renews with a recoat instead of a full tear-off; and a sealed, insulated, reflective roof eases heating and cooling load over its whole life.

Do you have to tear off the old roof first?

Usually not — spray foam bonds to most existing low-slope roofs after prep, so we can go right over what you have and skip the demolition and landfill fees. We'll assess yours first and tell you straight.

How does a spray foam roof lower a building's energy use?

Two ways: it air-seals and adds insulation across the whole roof, so less heat escapes in winter and less gets in during summer, and a reflective top coat bounces away solar heat. Together that means your heating and cooling systems don't have to work as hard over the life of the roof. Every building is different, so we won't promise a specific dollar figure — but that sealing, insulation, and reflectivity is what makes a foam roof efficient.
