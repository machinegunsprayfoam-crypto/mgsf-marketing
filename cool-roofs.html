How a cool roof works

Bounce the heat before it gets in

A dark roof soaks up the sun and dumps that heat into the building below — so your AC works harder all summer. A cool roof does the opposite: a bright, reflective coating bounces a big share of the sun's heat back off the roof before it ever gets inside. The building stays more comfortable, your cooling equipment doesn't grind as hard, and on a spray foam roof the reflective top coat protects the foam and reflects heat in the same layer.

Questions

Cool roofs — FAQ

What is a cool roof?

A cool roof has a bright, reflective coating that bounces the sun's heat back off the roof instead of soaking it in, so the building and the space under it stay cooler and the AC doesn't have to work as hard.

Can I put a cool-roof coating on my spray foam roof?

Yes — on a spray foam roof the reflective top coat does two jobs at once: it protects the foam from UV and weather and reflects heat, all in the same layer.
