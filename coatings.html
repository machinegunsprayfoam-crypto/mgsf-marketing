Why coat a roof

A coating is the wear layer that keeps a roof going

A sprayed or rolled elastomeric coating forms a seamless, flexible skin over your roof. It seals the seams, curbs and fasteners where leaks start, reflects UV to cut heat gain, and protects the layers underneath. When it ages, you clean and recoat the same surface — a renewable roof instead of a costly tear-off.

01 / SEAL

Seals leaks

Bridges seams, penetrations and fastener heads — the spots where metal, built-up and membrane roofs usually fail first.

02 / REFLECT

Reflective finish

Light-colored coatings bounce sunlight off the roof, easing heat load on shops, warehouses and ag buildings in summer.

03 / RENEW

Recoatable

Recoat at the end of the coating's service life instead of a full tear-off — the roof renews rather than going to a landfill.

Questions

Common questions

What kind of coatings do you apply?

Silicone and acrylic elastomeric coatings, applied to manufacturer spec. Silicone stands up to ponding water and UV on flat roofs; acrylic is a cost-effective, reflective option. We match the coating to your roof and climate.

Can you coat a metal roof?

Yes. A coating over a prepped metal roof seals rusting seams and fastener leaks and adds a reflective layer — a common fix for shop, barn and steel buildings in our region.

What is a recoatable roof?

The coating is the wear layer. When it reaches the end of its service life you clean and recoat the surface instead of tearing the roof off — the roof renews rather than going to a landfill. Call for specifics on your roof.
