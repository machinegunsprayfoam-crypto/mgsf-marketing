Questions

Common questions

Do you spray foam in cold Montana winters?

Yes. Northern Plains winters don't stop us — we check substrate temperature and dew point on every job so the foam bonds and cures correctly, with no callbacks.

Open-cell or closed-cell — which do I need?

Closed-cell for metal buildings, crawlspaces, roofs, and anywhere moisture and high R-value matter. Open-cell for interior walls and large attics where sound control and a vapor-permeable foam are the right fit. We'll tell you straight which one your job calls for.
