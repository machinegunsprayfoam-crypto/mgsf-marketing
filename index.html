What we do

Four ways we make your building work harder

01 / INSULATION

Spray Foam Insulation

Open and closed-cell foam that air-seals and insulates in one pass — walls, rim joists, metal shops, crawlspaces. Stops drafts and condensation cold.

02 / ROOFING

SPF Roofing & Coatings

Renew an aging metal or flat roof — seamless spray foam plus a silicone or acrylic topcoat. Seals every seam and adds insulation up top, often for less than a tear-off.

03 / CONCRETE

Concrete Lifting

Sunken driveway, garage slab, or sidewalk? We lift it back with polyurethane foam — no tear-out, cured in minutes, and we fill the voids so it stays put.

04 / DIAGNOSTICS

Blower-Door Energy Audits

BPI-certified testing that shows you exactly where a building leaks air — before you spend a dime on foam. Numbers first, no guesswork.

The honest pitch

Heating a leaky building is money out the roof

Most shops and older homes on the plains leak conditioned air through the rim joist, the walls, and a sweating metal roof. We run a blower-door test and show you the exact air leak first — then seal it with foam so your heat stays where you paid to put it. No guaranteed-savings promises; just real numbers and work that lasts.
