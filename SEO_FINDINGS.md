## What that means
Both domains have near-zero link authority. That's normal for a local contractor, and it's actually
**good news**: rankings here are won on **on-page relevance + local signals + a handful of citations**,
not on a big backlink war. The `.net` site is already strong on the on-page half (clean titles/meta,
FAQ schema, LocalBusiness/GeoCoordinates, full topic clusters, city pages). The missing levers are the
**off-page** ones below — none of which need a paid SEO tool.

## Highest-leverage SEO moves (no paid tool required)
1. **Pick ONE canonical domain.** Two near-identical live sites (`.info` + `.net`) split authority and
   risk duplicate content. 301-redirect one to the other so all signals compound on one domain.
2. **Google Business Profile** is the #1 local-ranking factor — claim/optimize it, post weekly, keep NAP
   identical to the site (2402 N Anderson Ave, Glendive MT / 406-939-8301). (Google weights GBP
   engagement heavily in 2026.)
3. **Local citations** — get listed with identical NAP on the big directories (Google, Bing Places,
   Apple, Yelp, Angi, BBB, HomeAdvisor) + MT/regional trade directories. Cheap authority.
4. **Reviews** — real Google reviews move both rank and conversion. (Homepage cards are placeholder
   until you paste real ones.)
5. **A few relevant backlinks** — supplier pages (ProFoam), local chamber, veteran-business directories
   (VOSB/SDVOSB), any press. Even a handful moves DR off 0.
